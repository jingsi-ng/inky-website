About inky · 印纪

A gift made from you, not just for you.

01 — Our Story

inky started with a question: what if a gift could be made from you, not just for you? We're a small studio in Dundalk, Ireland — a designer, a maker, and a dreamer — using algorithmic design and a UV+DTF printer to make things that last.

Every pattern begins as a name. The letters dissolve into lines, the lines become composition, the composition becomes an object you can hold. Something of you — your name, your date, your place — stays inside it permanently. That's what we make.

The name says it twice: inky for the ink, and 印纪 — yìn jì — “a printed record”.

02 — How the Generator Works

Deterministic, not random.

Our generator looks random, but it isn't. Behind every design is a simple promise: the same details always make the same pattern.

A

Your name is the seed

Your name, date, place and occasion are turned into a single number. That number decides where every brushstroke lands — so “Aoife” in wedding colours is always the same “Aoife”.

B

Letters become strokes

Each letter has a hand-drawn skeleton. The generator lays short, tapered brush marks along it, while faint symbols for your occasion — laurel for weddings, blossoms for birthdays, feathers for the finish line, stars for the team — rest underneath.

C

The seed lets you explore

The variation slider (1–99) nudges the arrangement without changing who it belongs to. Ninety-nine takes on the same name — pick the one that feels like you.

D

The code locks it in

Your order code — like INK-WE-AOIFE-12062027-DUN-01 — carries every detail. Send it to us and we regenerate your exact design at print resolution.
