what we believe

Something of you stays inside it.

inky started with a question: what if a gift could be made from you, not just for you? We're a small studio in Dundalk, Ireland — a designer, a maker, and a dreamer — using algorithmic design and a UV+DTF printer to make things that last.

Every pattern begins as a name. The letters dissolve into lines, the lines become a composition, the composition becomes an object you can hold. Something of you — your name, your date, your place — is fixed inside it permanently. That's what we make.

the generator

How your design is made

  1. 01

    Enter your details

    Your name, date, and place become the seed for everything.

  2. 02

    The algorithm runs

    Your name sets the shape — where every branch leans, where every ray starts. Then your details grow it: a leaf for every year, a ray for every kilometre. No two names produce the same composition.

  3. 03

    Choose your variation

    The seed slider lets you explore 99 different arrangements of the same pattern. Same name, different rhythm.

  4. 04

    Lock it with a code

    Your order code encodes your exact design. Share it with us and we reproduce it precisely, every time.

the occasions

Four occasions. Four visual languages.

One palette for everything we print — terracotta, cream and ink. Each occasion grows its own shapes instead.

💍

Wedding

Drawn from two trees that grew as one — love that endures

Grows with your years together: your wedding day starts the wreath, every year adds a leaf pair, and your guests become blossoms.

🎂

Birthday

Drawn from the spring flower — new beginnings, the joy of becoming

Grows with each birthday: a new ring of petals every ten years, a leaf for every year since.

🏃

Marathon / Run

Drawn from the laurel crown — the shape of a finish line earned

Grows with your run: every km adds a leaf pair (or a ray), your faster stretches turn terracotta, your time grows the laurel.

🏐

Match / Event

Drawn from the twin stars — two sides, one moment, always remembered

Grows with the score: twin stars for both sides, win or lose, and a small star for every point.
